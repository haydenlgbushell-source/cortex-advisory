Case Study

MB Auto Collective —
Before & After

A plain-English look at how a premium Sydney car dealership transformed its website, operations and online presence — and what that means for customers and the business.

Business MB Auto Collective
Location Waterloo, Sydney NSW
Established 2012
Speciality Prestige pre-owned vehicles
Engagement Full Build
01

First impressions — how the website looks

A website is your shopfront. For a premium dealership selling $50,000–$150,000 cars, the design needs to match the product. The old site didn’t do that. The new one does.

What a customer sees when they first visit
✕ Old website
✕Plain white background — looked like a free template anyone could download
✕No sense of luxury or prestige — didn’t match the calibre of cars being sold
✕Generic headline: “Buy | Sell | Drive” — nothing memorable
✓ New website
✓Dark, luxury design with gold accents — instantly signals “this is a premium business”
✓Looks comparable to prestige dealerships 10× the size
✓Opening line: “Drive what others only dream about” — sets the tone immediately

Customers decide within seconds whether a business is worth their time. The new website earns that trust before a single word is read.

02

Finding the right car — the buying experience

Customers shopping for prestige cars do their research online first. The easier you make that process, the more likely they are to pick up the phone.

Browsing and searching the car stock
✕ Old website
✕Basic list of cars — no way to filter or search
✕Limited photos and details shown on each car
✕No quick categories — had to scroll through everything
✓ New website
✓Filter by make, price range and body style instantly
✓Quick tabs: “Under 20,000 km”, “Convertibles”, “Finance Ready” and more
✓Each car shows full details — km, colour, fuel type, transmission and 20+ photos
What customers can do on the site
✕ Old website
✕View stock and contact — that was about it
✕No finance information available
✕No way to request a specific car that isn’t in stock
✓ New website
✓Get a valuation on your current car in under 2 minutes
✓“Source a Car” — Matt will search his network to find exactly what you want
✓Finance page explains your options clearly before you even visit
✓Book a viewing directly through the site — no phone tag required
03

Building trust — why customers feel confident

Buying a car from a private dealer is a big decision. Customers need to feel confident before they pick up the phone. The old site gave them no reason to. The new site gives them plenty.

Social proof and credibility signals
✕ Old website
✕No customer reviews shown anywhere on the site
✕Nothing to reassure someone who has never heard of the business
✕No indication of how long the business has been operating
✓ New website
✓126 five-star Google reviews shown prominently on the homepage
✓Real customer stories from buyers across Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane and Perth
✓“Est. 2012” — over a decade of experience visible straight away

“Couldn’t speak more highly of the service Matt offers. Knows the business inside out and stopped at nothing until we found the car we were after.” — Edward Simpson, Sydney

04

Being found on Google — reaching new customers

Most car buyers start with a Google search. If MB Auto Collective doesn’t appear in those results, the customer goes elsewhere. The new site is built to fix that.

What happens when someone searches online
✕ Old website
✕Hard to find unless you already knew the business name
✕Individual cars didn’t appear in Google search results at all
✕Page title just said “MB Auto Collective” — no keywords buyers actually search for
✓ New website
✓Each car has its own dedicated page that Google can find and display in results
✓Site now shows for searches like “prestige cars Sydney” and “used BMW Waterloo”
✓Price, kilometres and specs show directly in Google results — before a buyer even clicks
05

Behind the scenes — automation & the admin dashboard

The new website isn’t just what customers see — it’s also a powerful tool for running the business. A private admin dashboard lets Matt manage everything in one place, with AI doing the heavy lifting.

How social media posts used to work vs now
✕ The old way
✕Take photos of the car, write a caption from scratch
✕Log into Instagram, post manually — then log into Facebook, post again separately
✕Repeat for every single car — 30 to 60 minutes of work per vehicle
✓ The new way
✓Add the car once in the dashboard — AI writes captions for every platform automatically
✓Matt reviews and approves in one click — posts go out to all platforms at the best time of day
✓Under 5 minutes per vehicle — sold cars post automatically too
How it works, step by step
01

Matt adds a new car to the dashboard

He fills in the basics — make, model, year, price, kilometres, photos. No writing required at this stage.

02

AI does the research and writing

The system automatically looks up full specifications, checks what similar cars are selling for across Australia, and writes a professional listing description plus captions tailored for each social platform.

03

Matt reviews and approves

Everything lands in the dashboard for review. He can tweak the wording, adjust the price, or approve as-is. Nothing goes live without his say-so.

04

Posts go out across all platforms automatically

Once approved, the car goes live on the website and posts are sent at the optimal time to reach buyers.

Instagram Facebook TikTok LinkedIn
05

When a car sells — it handles that too

Mark the car as sold in the dashboard and it disappears from the website instantly. A “SOLD” post goes out to social media automatically, keeping the audience engaged and showing the business is active.

What else the dashboard manages
✉

Customer enquiries

Every contact form and valuation request submitted on the website comes straight into the dashboard — nothing gets lost in an inbox.

$

Live pricing intelligence

The system checks what similar cars are selling for across Australia so Matt can price competitively without hours of manual research.

≡

Sales briefings

For each car, the AI prepares a one-page summary — key selling points, common buyer questions, and who is most likely to buy it.

⏱

Viewing bookings

Customers book viewings directly on the site. Those bookings appear in the dashboard so Matt always knows who is coming in and when.

The system does the heavy lifting — writing, posting, researching prices — so Matt can spend that time with customers instead of behind a screen.

06

The bottom line — what changed for the business

Every improvement was made with one goal in mind: give MB Auto Collective the digital presence and tools that match the quality of the cars it sells and the service it delivers.

5 min to list a car and post
to all social media
(was 30–60 minutes)
126 five-star Google reviews
now visible to every visitor
4+ platforms updated
automatically per car

The old website did the basics but didn’t stand out. The new website looks the part, makes it easy for customers to find what they want, gives them more reasons to trust the business — and helps MB Auto Collective get found when people are searching Google for exactly what they sell. Behind the scenes, the automation means the business stays active online every day, without that activity costing hours of manual work.
