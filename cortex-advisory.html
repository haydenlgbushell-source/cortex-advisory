Foot in the Door

Start Fast.
Build Trust.
Expand.

Don’t pitch a transformation. Pitch a demo that solves something real this week. These are the five highest-conversion entry points.

01

The 15-Minute Newsletter Demo

Walk into a club meeting, take 3 paragraphs of their last member newsletter, and regenerate it in their voice in real time. Most clubs spend 3–4 hours on this every fortnight. You show them it takes 8 minutes.

→ Demo tool: Claude + few-shot prompt with club writing samples
02

Free Grant Application Audit

Offer to review a previous grant application and produce an AI-enhanced version. Community clubs apply for Sport Australia and council grants regularly — and mostly fail. Show them you can fix that, once, for free.

→ Upsell: $1,500–$3,000 grant writing retainer
03

Athlete Results → Automated Report

Take a meet or competition results spreadsheet and produce a structured performance summary document. For elite programs, this removes hours of coach admin after every competition. The demo writes itself.

→ Sells into: weekly reporting automation retainer
04

Social Content Week-in-a-Box

Generate a full week of social media posts from a single competition result and two athlete quotes. Show the content team what 45 minutes of work can become a 5-minute job.

→ Upsell: monthly content automation $600–$1,200/mo
05

AI Readiness Scoping Session (Paid)

For high-performance programs and larger clubs, offer a paid 90-minute AI scoping workshop. Identify the top 3 opportunities, quantify admin hours at stake, and present a prioritised roadmap. Price: $500–$1,500.

→ Leads directly to project or retainer proposal

How to Open the Conversation

Lead with time, not technology. These are the lines that land with sporting administrators, coaches, and club presidents.

For Community Clubs
“How many hours does your club spend on admin, newsletters, and social media every week? I can show you how to get that back.”
For High Performance Programs
“Your coaches are spending 30% of their time writing reports and emails. That’s time they’re not spending on athletes. I can fix that.”
For Grant-Dependent Clubs
“Most clubs leave grant money on the table because the application takes too long to write. I’ve built a system that can draft a full application in under an hour.”
For Coaching Businesses
“What would it mean for your business if every parent got a personalised weekly update on their child’s progress — without you writing a single word?”
